OUR STORY

Built on Experience Nobody Talks About

Cesar Olivarez learned construction the way most great craftsmen do — not in a classroom, but with his hands. Years of work across kitchens, bathrooms, framing, drywall, and emergency restoration built a level of expertise that simply cannot be taught. Every project he touches reflects that knowledge.

Mauricio Herazo brought something equally critical to the table: the organizational clarity that transforms skilled work into a reliable client experience. Clear timelines. Honest budgets. Communication that never leaves you guessing. Together, they built what Pennsylvania needed — a contractor with both the field knowledge and the professionalism to back it up.

In 2021, C Hawk Construction was born. Since then, more than 2000 completed projects — each one a promise kept.
